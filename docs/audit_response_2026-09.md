## 3. Precision@K versus MAP@K

The canonical result files carry only `map7_*`; `code/run_experiment.py` computes `map_at_k` and nothing
else. Adding Precision@K would be a new experiment (calibrators refitted on every arm; roughly 3–4 h of
compute once EB's arrays are all present). The user chose to keep the "no new numbers" discipline for
this revision, so the mismatch is answered by scope: Bayes-optimality is claimed for precision@K only
(Intro, Setup, Abstract), MAP@K is named as the benchmark metric and explicitly not the target of the
optimality statement, and the paper no longer contains any wording that suggests MAP-optimality.
Recorded as *not refuted, claim narrowed*.

## 8. Answers to the audit's eight reject reasons

1. **P@K theory vs MAP@K experiments** — wording scoped (§3 above). Not refuted; narrowed.
2. **Monotone-distortion assumption** — replaced by the operational definition (what a fitted per-label monotone map recovers) in every place it appeared.
3. **Ceiling gap attributed to saturation** — "is consistent with" everywhere; the tie-reordering experiment is the evidence, not an assumption.
4. **Test-split calibration** — the deployment number is now in the abstract and Table 1 caption; the test-split protocol is labelled diagnostic.
5. **Run dependence** — Fig. 1 caption states one matched pair and the draw variability; §3 separates the existence of the collapse (every draw) from its depth (`\nseedSraw`).
6. **float32 saturation** — separated from learner saturation and from coarse-leaf ties; raw-margin recommendation added.
7. **Scope** — unchanged Limitations paragraph on scope; claims elsewhere say "on our benchmarks".
8. **Information density** — offsetting cuts removed duplicated sentences (Intro/Related, Table 2 caption/§4, Contributions/Reproducibility) rather than evidence.

## Prediction arrays

The arrays the paper's numbers were computed from are pinned by SHA-256 in the `_meta.inputs` block of
every array-consuming result file. They were trained on two machines: the canonical uncapped matched
pairs, the Instacart cap sweep and the MLP arms on the training host; the Santander cap sweep, the
unweighted capped twins and the train-row draws on the host of this repository. They are being
consolidated on the latter through a shared transfer folder:

- `scripts/transfer_arrays.ps1` (run on the training host) copies each configuration's arrays to the
  transfer folder with a `TRANSFER.json` manifest;
- `scripts/receive_arrays.py` (run on the receiving host) recomputes every SHA-256, compares it with the
  canonical hash and places matching files;
- `scripts/pack_arrays_for_zenodo.py --dry-run` lists which pinned arrays are still missing or mismatched
  locally, and without `--dry-run` assembles the complete set for a Zenodo dataset record.

The scripts read their locations from `ODDSLIP_ARRAYS_ROOT` (the local arrays root),
`ODDSLIP_TRANSFER_DIR` (the transfer folder) and `ODDSLIP_ZENODO_DIR` (the upload directory), or from
explicit arguments.

Known gaps at the time of writing: the Instacart all-product MLP arrays (`p_te_all.npy`, `p_va_all.npy`
under `instacart_product/outputs_mlp/{w1,wr}`) are not yet in the transfer folder; the 100-tree unweighted
Santander scorer (`outputs_lgbm_baseline/predictions.npz`) is DVC-tracked in the training repository and
was restored with `dvc pull`.

The `retrain_check` block in `santander_capsweep.json` records that retraining the Santander uncapped pair
on the receiving host, with the same data, script, configuration and LightGBM version, did not reproduce
the canonical arrays bit for bit (weighted raw MAP@7 0.111 against 0.117; unweighted 0.808 against 0.808).
The paper's Limitations state this as run-to-run variation across machines; the canonical arrays themselves
are intact on the training host. (The `note` string inside that JSON block was written before this was
understood and still says "lost"; the numbers around it are unaffected and the file is left as generated.)
